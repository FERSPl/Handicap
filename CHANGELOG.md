## v1.7.0 Changelog

### *What's new?*
- **New Handicaps**
  - Added `flatulence`: you randomly fart.
  - Added `stiff_neck`: you can barely look up or down.
  - Added `double_vision`: you see everything twice, slightly shifted.
- **New Versions**
  - Handicap is now available for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric, Quilt and NeoForge) and 1.20.1 (Fabric, Quilt and Forge).
  - In 1.21.1 and 1.20.1, only one screen filter is displayed at a time, like in 26.1 and 26.2.
- **Sounds**
  - A discreet sound now plays when you get a new handicap, along with the action bar message.

### *Changes & Balancing*
- **Schizophrenia**
  - More fake sounds: an exploding creeper, a zombie breaking a door, a skeleton shooting, a teleporting enderman, angry piglins, witches and phantoms.
  - Fake sounds now happen more often than fake chat messages.
